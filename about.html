About

We built the layer we kept having to build by hand.

CloakShield Pro is a security layer for teams already routing traffic through Cloaking House, Keitaro, Voluum or something home-grown. It scores the click before your router sees it, and it watches the page after the click lands.

Why it exists

In 2021 the four of us were running acquisition for a payments client and losing about a fifth of the budget to traffic that was never going to convert — scraper fleets, compliance sweeps, residential proxy pools rented by the hour. The router did its job perfectly. It just had no idea what it was routing.

So we wrote a scoring service in front of it. Then we wrote it again for the next client, and again for the one after that, each time re-deriving the same ASN lists, the same TLS fingerprint checks, the same timezone-versus-carrier comparison. The fourth rewrite became the product.

The design constraint has never changed: we sit beside your stack, not on top of it. If you turn CloakShield Pro off tomorrow, your campaigns keep running. That is deliberate — a filtering layer you cannot remove is a filtering layer you cannot trust.

How we run it

  • Verdicts are computed across 38 edge PoPs. The p95 is 7.2 ms; the median is 2.4 ms.
  • Every verdict is logged with the signal that produced it. No score without a stated reason.
  • We store how a request was scored, never your offers, creatives or page contents.
  • Signal lists — ASN ranges, datacenter blocks, known crawler fingerprints — update every 30 minutes.
  • Rules ship behind a shadow mode first, so you see what a change would have filtered before it filters anything.
  • Two engineers carry the pager. Neither of them is an autoresponder.
Principles

Four commitments we will not trade away

Every verdict is explainable

If we filter a click, the log says which signal decided it — the ASN, the fingerprint mismatch, the request cadence. A black-box score is indistinguishable from a coin flip you cannot audit.

Your content stays yours

We hold configuration, verdicts and integrity hashes. We do not store your landing pages, your creatives or your offer terms, so a breach of us is not a breach of your campaign strategy.

Leaving is one DNS record

Onboarding is a CNAME. Offboarding is deleting it. There is no proprietary tag buried in your funnel and no migration project waiting for you at the end of a contract.

Pricing that does not punish success

Plans are priced per month, not per click. A campaign that suddenly works should not generate a surprise invoice, and you should never have to choose between filtering traffic and affording to filter traffic. The multi-month discounts on the pricing page are the whole discount structure — there is no enterprise pricing conversation where the real numbers live.
