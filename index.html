Platform

A filtering layer in front of the router you already run

CloakShield scores the request, resolves the real geography and checks the landing page is still the page you published — then hands the visitor to your existing flow untouched.

Bot filtering

Four independent signals score every click — ASN reputation, TLS fingerprint, headless indicators and request cadence. A verdict lands in 7.2 ms at p95.

Geo resolution

Country, region and carrier resolved from GeoIP2 and an ASN lookup, then cross-checked against language and timezone. A mismatch is marked proxied, not trusted.

Page integrity

Every landing page is fetched from 30 vantage points each minute and diffed against what you published. Injected scripts and dead offer links raise an alert with the diff.

Funnel masking

Response-level masking rotates class names and DOM structure per session, so competitive-intelligence crawlers and spy tools cannot fingerprint your templates.

Platform agnostic

Click parameters — sub1–sub10, clickid, cost — are forwarded verbatim and postbacks replayed in the format your platform already accepts.

One record to switch on

One CNAME and an API key, or a single script tag if you would rather not touch DNS. Rolling back is deleting the record — we hold no campaign state of our own.

How it works

From DNS record to filtered traffic in four steps

Point

Add one CNAME for your campaign domain. Your router keeps its address and its rules — nothing is migrated.

Score

Every click is scored at the nearest edge PoP. A verdict lands in 7.2 ms at p95, and a timeout passes the visitor through.

Hand off

Clean traffic reaches your platform with parameters untouched and the verdict attached, so existing flows decide as they do now.

Watch

Landing pages are diffed every minute against the version you published, and tampering raises an alert with the diff attached.

Compatibility

Runs in front of your cloaker, not in place of it

Cloaking House, Keitaro, Voluum and the rest keep every campaign, flow, stream and offer where it is today. There is no migration step to plan.

Cloaking House Keitaro Voluum Binom RedTrack
CNAME · pre-filter

Point the campaign domain at a CloakShield edge PoP and leave Cloaking House as the decision layer behind it. The verdict travels as request headers — x-cs-score, x-cs-asn, x-cs-geo — alongside the untouched query string, so your white-page and offer rules read an enriched request instead of a bare IP.

  • Verdict headers on every forwarded request
  • White-page and offer rules stay as written
  • One DNS record to add, the same one to remove
REST import · S2S postback

Campaigns, streams and offer IDs import over /admin_api/v1/campaigns, so identifiers map one-to-one instead of being retyped into a second console. Conversions are replayed to the postback URL you already have on file, in the format Keitaro accepts. Self-hosted and cloud instances behave identically.

  • Flow, stream and offer IDs map 1:1
  • Click parameters forwarded unmodified
  • Postbacks replayed in Keitaro's own format
Custom domain · click passthrough

CloakShield answers on your tracking domain and hands the visitor to Voluum's redirect endpoint with cid intact, so click IDs, cost data and the conversion chain still reconcile at the end of the day. Traffic source templates, path weights and offer caps stay configured in Voluum.

  • Click ID and cost survive the extra hop
  • Path weights and caps stay in Voluum
  • Filtered clicks logged with the deciding signal
Self-hosted · direct integration

Binom runs on your own server, so the filter sits in front of that server rather than in front of a vendor. Campaign and landing IDs import over the public API on your instance, and the click is handed on with clickid and every &s1..s20 token untouched, which is what keeps Binom's own reports whole.

  • Campaign and landing IDs import over the API
  • All twenty s-parameters forwarded intact
  • No change to your server or its reports
API key · conversion API

An API key is enough to pull offers, sources and campaigns across. Filtered clicks are reported back through RedTrack's conversion API with a cost of zero and a status that marks them as rejected, so the traffic never inflates a payout report while still leaving an audit trail against the source that sent it.

  • Offers, sources and campaigns pulled by API key
  • Rejected clicks reported back, not silently dropped
  • Attribution windows and payouts stay in RedTrack

FAQ

Questions we get on the first call

Anything else, ask support — a human answers.

Do we have to move our campaigns into CloakShield?
No, and there is nowhere to move them to. CloakShield holds no campaign state — your flows, streams, offers and split weights stay in Cloaking House, Keitaro, Voluum or whatever you run. We add one hop in front of it, and removing that hop is deleting a DNS record.
What does the extra hop do to latency?
Scoring happens at the nearest of 38 edge PoPs, so the visitor is not routed across the world to reach us. The added cost is 7.2 ms at p95 and about 2.4 ms at the median. If the scorer times out the request is passed straight through rather than dropped — a bad minute on our side never costs you a click.
What decides whether a click gets filtered?
Four independent signals: ASN and datacenter reputation across 41,300 published ranges, a JA4 TLS fingerprint checked against the declared User-Agent, headless indicators such as navigator.webdriver and canvas hash collisions, and request cadence per /24. Thresholds are yours to tune, and every verdict is logged with the signal that decided it, so you can audit a call you disagree with.
Do I need an account before I can subscribe?
Yes. Registration comes first: you tell us whether you are buying as an individual, a sole proprietor or a company, confirm your email address, and land in the workspace. The plan you chose on this page is carried through, and billing is activated from inside the workspace rather than from the marketing site.
What counts as a filtered click?
One scored request through the edge, whether it ends up passed or stopped. Landing page integrity checks, dashboard views, API reads and postback replays are not metered. If you go over, nothing breaks — you are billed for the overage at the same rate rather than throttled mid-campaign.
How long does setup actually take?
One CNAME and an API key, or a single script tag if you would rather not touch DNS — about 15 minutes. Existing campaigns import over the Keitaro and Voluum REST APIs, so flows and offer IDs map across instead of being retyped. Most buyers have a first campaign running through it the same afternoon.
Can we cancel or change plans mid-term?
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any point and stay active to the end of the paid period. Upgrades take effect immediately with the remaining balance credited. Prepaid multi-month terms can be upgraded at any time; downgrades apply at renewal.
What do you store about our traffic, and where?
Click verdicts and the signals behind them, plus integrity results and your own configuration, in the region you select at signup — US, EU or AP. We do not store offer data, creatives or the contents of your pages. Raw request logs are kept 30 days for abuse investigation; Enterprise plans can pin every component to a single jurisdiction.
