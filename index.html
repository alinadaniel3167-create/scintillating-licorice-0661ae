The platform

A filtering layer in front of the router you already run

CloakShield Pro sits between the click and your traffic routing platform. It scores the request, resolves the real geography, and checks the landing page is still the page you published — then hands the visitor to your existing flow untouched.

Universal compatibility

Works in front of any traffic routing platform — Cloaking House, Keitaro, Voluum, Binom, RedTrack or a router you wrote yourself. CloakShield reads the same click parameters your platform expects (sub1–sub10, clickid, cost), forwards them verbatim, and replays postbacks in the format each platform already accepts. Filtering happens on the edge PoP before the request reaches your router, so your routing rules never change.

Enhanced security

Bot filtering scores every request on four independent signals: ASN and datacenter reputation across 41,300 published ranges, a JA4 TLS fingerprint checked against the declared User-Agent, headless indicators (navigator.webdriver, absent WebGL vendor string, canvas hash collisions), and request cadence per /24. A verdict lands in 7.2 ms at p95 and fails open — if the scorer times out, the visitor still reaches your router.

One record to switch on

Setup is one CNAME and an API key, or a single script tag if you would rather not touch DNS. Existing campaigns import over the Keitaro and Voluum REST APIs, so flows, streams and offer IDs map one-to-one instead of being retyped. Rolling back is deleting the CNAME record.

Advanced masking

Geo routing resolves country, region and carrier from MaxMind GeoIP2 plus an ASN lookup, then cross-checks it against the Accept-Language header and reported timezone — a mismatch marks the session as proxied rather than silently trusting the IP. Response-level masking rotates class names and DOM structure per session so competitive-intelligence crawlers and spy tools cannot fingerprint your funnel templates.

Campaign protection

Landing page integrity checks fetch every page from 30 vantage points each minute, hash the DOM with known-dynamic nodes excluded, and diff loaded third-party scripts against an allowlist. Injected redirects, expired offer links, lapsed TLS and defacement raise an alert with the diff attached — not a generic uptime ping.

Platform agnostic

Nothing here asks you to leave the routing solution you already know. CloakShield holds no campaign state of its own, stores no offer data, and adds one hop you can remove at any time — filtering, geo resolution and integrity results are written back as parameters and postbacks your platform already understands.

How it works

From DNS record to filtered traffic in four steps

The first three take an afternoon. The fourth runs on its own from then on.

Point

Add one CNAME for your campaign domain, or drop in a script tag if you would rather not touch DNS. Your router keeps its current address and its current rules — nothing is migrated.

Score

Every click is scored at the nearest edge PoP on ASN reputation, TLS fingerprint, headless indicators and request cadence. A verdict lands in 7.2 ms at p95, and a scorer timeout passes the visitor through rather than dropping them.

Hand off

Clean traffic reaches your platform with the click parameters untouched and the verdict attached, so your existing flows and split tests carry on deciding exactly as they do now.

Watch

Landing pages are fetched from 30 vantage points a minute and diffed against the version you published. Tampering, injected redirects, dead offer links and expiring TLS raise an alert with the diff attached.

Compatible platforms

Runs in front of your cloaker, not in place of it

CloakShield Pro is a filtering hop, not a router. Cloaking House, Keitaro, Voluum and the rest keep every campaign, flow, stream and offer exactly where it is today. CloakShield scores the click first, then hands it over with the parameters your platform already reads — which is why there is no migration step to plan.

Cloaking House

CNAME · pre-filter

Point the campaign domain at a CloakShield edge PoP and leave Cloaking House as the decision layer behind it. The verdict travels as request headers — x-cs-score, x-cs-asn, x-cs-geo — alongside the untouched query string, so your existing white-page and offer rules read an enriched request instead of a bare IP. Filter lists you have already tuned keep running; ours runs earlier and quietly absorbs what yours would have had to judge.

  • Verdict headers on every forwarded request

Keitaro

REST import · S2S postback

Campaigns, streams and offer IDs import over /admin_api/v1/campaigns, so identifiers map one-to-one and nothing is retyped into a second console. sub1–sub10, clickid and cost are forwarded verbatim, and conversions are replayed to the postback URL you already have on file, in the format Keitaro accepts. Self-hosted and cloud instances behave identically; the only difference is where the API key points.

  • Flow, stream and offer IDs map 1:1
  • Click parameters forwarded unmodified
  • Postbacks replayed in Keitaro's own format

FAQ

Questions we get on the first call

Anything else, ask support — a human answers.

Do we have to move our campaigns into CloakShield?
No, and there is nowhere to move them to. CloakShield holds no campaign state — your flows, streams, offers and split weights stay in Cloaking House, Keitaro, Voluum or whatever you run. We add one hop in front of it, and removing that hop is deleting a DNS record.
What does the extra hop do to latency?
Scoring happens at the nearest of 38 edge PoPs, so the visitor is not routed across the world to reach us. The added cost is 7.2 ms at p95 and about 2.4 ms at the median. If the scorer times out the request is passed straight through rather than dropped — a bad minute on our side never costs you a click.
What decides whether a click gets filtered?
Four independent signals: ASN and datacenter reputation across 41,300 published ranges, a JA4 TLS fingerprint checked against the declared User-Agent, headless indicators such as navigator.webdriver and canvas hash collisions, and request cadence per /24. Thresholds are yours to tune, and every verdict is logged with the signal that decided it, so you can audit a call you disagree with.
What counts as a filtered click?
One scored request through the edge, whether it ends up passed or stopped. Landing page integrity checks, dashboard views, API reads and postback replays are not metered. If you go over, nothing breaks — you are billed for the overage at the same rate rather than throttled mid-campaign.
How long does setup actually take?
One CNAME and an API key, or a single script tag if you would rather not touch DNS — about 15 minutes. Existing campaigns import over the Keitaro and Voluum REST APIs, so flows and offer IDs map across instead of being retyped. Most buyers have a first campaign running through it the same afternoon.
Can we cancel or change plans mid-term?
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any point and stay active to the end of the paid period. Upgrades take effect immediately with the remaining balance credited. Prepaid multi-month terms can be upgraded at any time; downgrades apply at renewal.
What do you store about our traffic, and where?
Click verdicts and the signals behind them, plus integrity results and your own configuration, in the region you select at signup — US, EU or AP. We do not store offer data, creatives or the contents of your pages. Raw request logs are kept 30 days for abuse investigation; Enterprise plans can pin every component to a single jurisdiction.
